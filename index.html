The story so far

I'm Harini, and I'm in the first year of B.Tech Information Technology. A few months ago I could only use computers. Now I'm learning to tell them what to do, and honestly, it's more fun than I expected.

Most of what I know so far came from a mix of classes, late-night tutorials and a lot of "why is this not working?" I'm still near the beginning, and I'm okay with that. Being new means everything is still interesting.
